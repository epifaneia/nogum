THE SHADOW OVER INNSMOUTH · Massachusetts, winter of 1927

Innsmouth was once a rich port. Today it is a town of sunken roofs, rotting wharves and boarded-up windows. Nobody from Arkham or Newburyport goes there if they can help it, and the only bus that runs there is driven by a man no one has ever seen blink.

The locals have "the Innsmouth look": bulging eyes that hardly close, rough skin, odd creases along the sides of the neck. They walk with a strange rolling gait and say little. Nearly all of them belong to the Esoteric Order of Dagon, which has held the old Masonic hall ever since Captain Obed Marsh came back from the South Seas with gold… and with something else.

Off the coast rises Devil Reef. On moonless nights there are lights above it, and boats leave the harbour with their lanterns out.

Three weeks ago a Miskatonic University student who had gone to sketch the town's architecture disappeared in Innsmouth. The county police want nothing to do with it. His family has hired a detective from Boston.

Loose leads:
— The Marsh refinery still smelts gold, though nobody knows where it comes from.
— Zadok Allen, the oldest drunk in town, will talk for a bottle. Nobody believes him.
— At the Gilman House hotel the walls are thin. Too thin.
— The grocery is run by a boy from Arkham. He's the only one who tells anything, and he wants out.
