Bazaar index by category

The catalogue is ordered by world, because that is how tone reads. This is the other door: the same objects ordered by what they are and what they cost, for when what you are after is an Access 1 weapon and you do not care which setting it comes from. The category is a reading aid and not a rule: an exoskeleton is both vehicle and defence, and it sits where it gets used most. Within each category they go from least to most Access, and at the same Access, whatever costs the most Keys and points comes first; whatever carries no Access, the powers, goes at the end. The prices are the catalogue’s, for a level 1 bearer (how to read a stat block). C·e5
