  • Total bonus = skill + difficulty + equipment + effects. Difficulty is a modifier, the TN does not move: +2 easy · 0 standard · −2 hard · −4 heroic.

The Grid ch. 3 · 9.5

One level per Sphere, in the Sphere of the action. One level is enough (3.1):

  • Same level: you roll as normal.
  • The one below has no right to roll, either to hit or to save themselves: their defeat is narrated. Unless they bring a Key.
  • The one above does not roll: they succeed. If how matters, Style.

The two readings, depending on the tone (3.2). Narrative: the one above is what the story is about; its own scale, or none. Tangible: they really can do a hundred times more.

The Key ch. 4 · 9.5

A whole Discipline (Athletics, Acrobatics, Stealth…), against any target, puts you n Grid levels above your own. On that roll you act at that Grid (4.1).

  • You are a 1 against a 1: it puts you at 2 and they do not roll. Against a 2: you roll, with your full bonus; nothing is protected twice.
  • It cancels what it says it cancels, and nothing more (4.2). The grenade, Key 2: it cannot be soaked, it is dodged at −2, and it can hurt a level-2 creature.
  • One Key per trait; the same concept is not charged twice.

The tally ch. 5

Two purses that do not exchange: points (craft) and Keys (size). Equipment also asks for Access: what your Resources let you have (0 everyday · 1 serious weapon · 2 military · 3 what should not exist).

  • Start: 12 points (14 for everyone if any player takes Arcane) and a separate pot: 5 Keys or +1 Grid. Recommended starting point, 1·1·1·1·2, with the 2 wherever the universe allows. More or less, at session zero (5.2).
  • Progress: by default, one Key per session per head; points, at the table’s pace (5.8).

Combat ch. 6

  • The Grid first: at different levels, the one below does not roll.
  • Initiative by guards: declare Violence, Trick or Speciality and roll your best matching skill. Highest first; on a tie, higher bonus, then the PCs. Critical, first; fumble, last. Changing guard: at the end (6.1).
  • 1 · Impact: the attacker rolls against the TN. Fumble or failure, no hit: there is no defence. Success, it hits. Critical, +1 to damage.
  • 2 · Mitigation: if the stat block allows it, the defender chooses to dodge (Acrobatics) or soak (Fortitude) and rolls against the TN. Compare result levels (6.2):

  • Damage: 1 light, 2 serious. A weapon’s reaches 2, plus the critical; above that, the Reaper.
  • Tags, which override the tie (6.3). Dodge: Full · Partial (at least 1) · Hard −2 · Easy +2 · None. Soak: Full · Max N · None. Grid N: the weapon is a Key of that level.
  • Fractal health, the same sheet: 3 blocks (Lethal) · 9 boxes (Tactical) · 27 points (War). Healthy, Wounded, Critical, Out of the fight (6.4).
  • Bars: Physical, Mental (the social goes here), Spiritual and the genre’s own. Their penalties add up. They come back whole once you are safe; consequences stay until healed (6.5).
  • Out of the fight is not death: death is agreed (0.7).
